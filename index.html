Built on Arc · USDC-native

An AI agent runs your freelance contracts, inside spending limits that a smart contract enforces.

Foreman reviews milestone deliveries, pays for its own research with tiny USDC vouchers, and approves, rejects or escalates. The limits live on-chain, so a confused or compromised model still cannot move more money than you allowed.

  1. Escrow holds the money. The agent never holds job funds.
  2. Agent investigates. It buys evidence per call from a priced tool catalog.
  3. Contract checks the limits. Per-approval cap, daily cap, kill switch.
  4. Every decision is hash-committed. Check it yourself below.
